Produce the Friday ROMAS Read.

1. Invoke `friday-read-editor`.
2. Read `friday_read_history.json` to determine this week's rubric (rotate: Week in Receipts → Five Things That Shifted → What I Got Wrong → Watch Next Week).
3. If rubric is "Week in Receipts", load `friday_read_predictions.json` from last week's "Watch Next Week" for scoring.
4. Draft the issue (2,000–3,500 words):
   - Standfirst, lead, 3–5 sections with primary-source-linked claims, ROMAS Take per section, Bottom Line.
   - Sign-off `— Kimal`.
5. Annotate claims for `clinical-fact-checker`.
6. Decide audio handoff: 10-min Audio Brief or 30–60 min Podcast.
7. Hand off audio target to `audio-producer`.
8. Surface to Kimal for sign-off pre-lock.
