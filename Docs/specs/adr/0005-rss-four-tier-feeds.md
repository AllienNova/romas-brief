## Context

ROMAS Wire produces audio content across four distinct formats with different cadences, audiences, and distribution needs:

| Tier | Format | Cadence | Launch (cycle-3 lock 2026-05-14) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — ROMAS Audio Brief | Per-article, 5/7/10 min | Per article (daily) | **Day 1** |
| 2 — ROMAS Daily Brief | Daily roundup, 10–15 min | Daily | **Day 1** |
| 3 — The ROMAS Audio Podcast | Weekly deep-dive, 30–60 min | Weekly | **Day 1** — episode 001 full 30–60 min episode ships per Q2-A lock |
| 4 — ROMAS Conference Brief | Conference live, 15–30 min | During ASTRO/ESTRO/AAPM/JASTRO/RANZCR | **Day 1 feed live, first activation per conference window** |
| 5 — ROMAS Video Podcast (new tier) | With invited human guest, 30–60 min | Weekly or bi-weekly TBD | **Day 60** — see ADR-0012 (Video hosting vendor) |

Podcast apps (Apple Podcasts, Spotify, Overcast, Pocket Casts) subscribe to RSS 2.0 + `<itunes:*>` namespace feeds. A subscriber to the Audio Brief feed does not want daily roundup episodes mixed in; a subscriber to the Podcast feed expects weekly deep-dives only. If formats share a feed, subscribers receive unexpected episode types and churn.

The revoke workflow (ADR-0006) requires RSS regeneration per-feed when an episode is revoked — a per-tier feed makes this surgical. A single combined feed would require regenerating the entire feed on any revoke event.

---

## Decision

Maintain **four independent RSS feeds**, one per tier:

| Feed file | Tier | `audio_jobs.audio_tier` filter (ADR-0017) | Day-1 status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `audio-brief.xml` | ROMAS Audio Brief | `audio_tier = 'audio_brief'` | Live with ~30 episodes |
| `daily-brief.xml` | ROMAS Daily Brief | `audio_tier = 'daily_brief'` | Live with ~5 rehearsal episodes |
| `podcast.xml` | ROMAS Audio Podcast | `audio_tier = 'podcast'` | Live with episode 001 (full 30–60 min) + ~9 buffer episodes |
| `conference-brief.xml` | ROMAS Conference Brief | `audio_tier = 'conference_brief'` | Live with ~5 historical episodes (ASTRO/ESTRO/AAPM 2025 + 2 prior) |
| `video-podcast.xml` (new, cycle-3 + cycle-5) | ROMAS Video Podcast — Tier 5 | `audio_tier = 'video_podcast'` (schema enum extension TBD) | Skeleton-only Day 1; populated Day 60 per ADR-0012 |

Each feed is generated by `workers/rss-publisher` on:
- New `audio_status = published` event for any `audio_jobs` row of that tier.
- `audio_status = revoked` event (episode removed from feed, feed regenerated).

Feeds are served from `romas-audio-cdn` R2 bucket (public) at stable URLs. CDN TTL for feeds: 60s (must reflect revokes promptly).

The index at `audio_jobs.audio_tier` + `audio_status = published` (cms-schema.md:114; column renamed per ADR-0017) backs feed generation queries.

**Podcast timeline — LOCKED 2026-05-14 by Kimal (cycle-3 + cycle-3 Q2-A)**: ALL FOUR audio tiers launch **Day 1**, all via ElevenLabs primary + PlayHT failover. Tier 3 Audio Podcast episode 001 ships Day 1 as a full 30–60 min episode (per Q2-A). Tier 5 Video Podcast (new) launches Day 60 per ADR-0012 placeholder. The cycle-1 "Day 14 / Day 30–45" wording in CLAUDE.md §3 row 6 + AGENT.md §3 phase 8 is SUPERSEDED by SSOT §3 row 6 + §10 Q2 + Q2-A; the M0 doc-reconciliation cycle propagates the cycle-3 lock through CLAUDE.md + AGENT.md + Master Strategy in the same PR as this ADR refresh.

A fifth feed `video-podcast.xml` is reserved Day 1 (skeleton present, no episodes) and populates at Day 60.

## Alternatives Considered

### Single feed with tier tags (`<itunes:season>` or custom namespace)

Rejected. Podcast apps do not filter on custom tags — subscribers would receive all episode types regardless of their intent. Apple Podcasts and Spotify have no native tier-filtering UI. A Tier-3 subscriber would receive 5 Audio Brief episodes per day plus the weekly podcast episode in a single undifferentiated stream. Subscriber churn from feed pollution is a real risk in a specialized clinical audience.

### Per-article feeds (one RSS file per article)

Rejected. Per-article feeds cannot be listed in podcast directories (Apple Podcasts requires a single feed per show). Subscribers cannot discover the show through a single subscription link. Feed management complexity (thousands of XML files for 500+ articles) adds unnecessary storage and cache invalidation overhead. No podcast app has a UX for per-article feed subscriptions.

### Single feed split by subscription tier (paid/free)

Not applicable at launch. ROMAS Wire has no paid-gated content at launch. If a paid tier is introduced, a subscriber-authenticated feed variant may be warranted — that would be a new ADR, not a change to this one.

## Consequences

**Positive**
- Listeners subscribe to exactly the format they want. Audio Brief subscribers do not see Daily Brief or Podcast episodes.
- Revoke operations are surgical: revoking one Audio Brief episode regenerates only `audio-brief.xml`, not the other three feeds.
- Conference Brief feed (`conference-brief.xml`) can go dormant between conferences without affecting other feeds. `conference-mode-operator` activates it by publishing the first episode.
- Each feed can have independent `<itunes:category>`, `<itunes:explicit>`, and description metadata tuned per format.
- Podcast directory submission (Apple Podcasts, Spotify) is straightforward: one feed URL per show type.

**Negative**
- Four feed files to validate and maintain. A `workers/rss-publisher` bug affects all four unless per-tier tests cover each.
- Listeners who want all content must subscribe to multiple feeds. No aggregated "everything ROMAS Wire" feed exists — intentional but worth documenting.
- Conference Brief feed is inactive between conferences; podcast apps may flag it as stale if no episodes publish for extended periods. Mitigation: publish a "season pause" episode or update `<lastBuildDate>` periodically.

**Neutral**
- Feed file names (`audio-brief.xml` etc.) are locked as public URLs. Renaming them after subscribers register in podcast apps would break existing subscriptions. Treat feed URLs as stable public API contracts.

---

## Revisit Triggers

- A podcast directory (Apple Podcasts, Spotify) changes its feed validation rules in a way that breaks one of the four feeds — evaluate consolidation at that point if the fix is impractical.
- A fifth audio format is introduced (e.g., "ROMAS Grand Rounds" — monthly deep-dives) — add `0005b` ADR for the new tier rather than amending this one.
- Subscriber analytics (Plausible + feed download logs) show that fewer than 5% of subscribers use more than one feed — evaluate whether the multi-feed complexity is justified.

---

## Historical Context

The four-tier audio architecture was specified in CLAUDE.md §5 and AGENT.md §2 from planning-kit inception (2026-05-12), with explicit RSS feed filenames (`audio-brief.xml`, `daily-brief.xml`, `podcast.xml`, `conference-brief.xml`) named as public URL contracts. No alternative feed structure was compared at decision time. The retroactive ratification here is honest: a single-feed-with-tier-tags model would have forced subscribers who want only the Daily Brief to download every Audio Brief + Conference Brief; it would also have prevented per-feed iTunes/podcast namespace customization (only `podcast.xml` carries it). The four-feed structure trades one-time RSS infrastructure complexity for ongoing subscriber experience clarity. Per-article feeds (rejected alternative) would have produced thousands of feeds — operationally absurd.

*Accepted retroactively 2026-05-14 per CLAUDE.md §5. Feed file names locked as public URL contracts. Historical Context added cycle-2 per critic F-P1-05.*
